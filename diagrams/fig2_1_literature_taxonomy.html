Taxonomy of Related Work & Research Evolution

Comparative Mapping of Theoretical Foundations, Interface Adaptation Paradigms, and Proposed Architecture

1. Static & Probabilistic Keyboards
MacKenzie & Soukoreff (2002), Goodman (2002)
  • Bivariate Gaussian Modeling: Analyzed 2D touch endpoint distributions across standard QWERTY keys.
  • Bigram / N-Gram Priors: Linguistic context disambiguates boundary touch landings.
  • Autocorrect Reliance: Errors fixed retroactively after word completion.
Limitation: Static hitboxes; cannot adapt to thumb reach drift or individual hand anatomy.
2. Spatial Personalization
Sivek & Riley (2022) [Gboard]
  • On-Device User Profiles: Models consistent individual touch offsets (X-Y centroids).
  • Postural Awareness: Compensates for one-handed thumb vs. two-thumb rotational variance.
  • Significant CER Reduction: Proved personalized spatial decoders outperform static bounds.
Limitation: Uses offline gradient batching; lacks active online reinforcement feedback loops.
3. Ability-Based & Assistive HCI
Mitchell et al. (2022), Aurenhammer (1987)
  • Ability-Based Design: Interfaces mold to user capabilities rather than forcing user conformity.
  • Tremor Accommodation: Expanding target bounds for users with motor impairments.
  • Power Diagrams: Laguerre-Voronoi math enables non-overlapping space partitioning.
Limitation: Often requires tedious manual user calibration or static profiling sessions.
4. Human-in-the-Loop RL
Gao (2022) [UC Berkeley], Sutton & Barto (2018)
  • Interaction as Environment: State incorporates user cadence, coordinates, and error history.
  • Implicit Reward Feedback: Backspaces and error rates drive policy improvement.
  • Continuous Policy Adaptation: Learns without pre-labeled offline training datasets.
Limitation: Visual instability risk if layout transforms too aggressively during typing.

Proposed Solution: Actor-Critic Adaptive Keyboard with Laguerre-Voronoi Tessellation

Unifies continuous reinforcement learning (Actor-Critic / DQN / PPO) with Laguerre-Voronoi power diagrams and Bayesian bigram priors. Enforces subtle geometric hitbox adaptations while preserving visual QWERTY stability and penalizing excessive layout shifts.
